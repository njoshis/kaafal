Kaafal Family Pack is a multifloral raw honey from a single apiary in the Kumaon region of Uttarakhand, at approximately 1,500m elevation. The bees forage on wildflowers, kafal blossoms, eucalyptus, and seasonal flora from the surrounding hillsides.

Every jar carries the batch number, apiary GPS coordinates, harvest month, and a QR code linking to the full NMR test report for that batch. We publish every report - not just the ones that look good.

Warm and floral with mild woody undertones. Natural sweetness without the sharp sugar edge of processed honey. Slightly thick in consistency, with a warm amber colour that lightens as it crystallises.

Raw honey naturally crystallises over time - this is a sign of quality, not spoilage. Place the jar in warm water (under 40°C) to return it to liquid without damaging any nutrients.

This batch is from a migratory Apis mellifera apiary that overwinters in the Kumaon hills before moving to the Bareilly-Baheri belt for litchi season and the Rajasthan mustard belt in winter. Every move is logged. Every location is on the website.

The Kumaon harvest happens March through May, when the hills are in full blossom. This is the multifloral window - kafal, wildflowers, and early eucalyptus all contributing to the hive at the same time.
