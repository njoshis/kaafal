The Name

Named for a berry that only grows here.

Kaafal (Myrica esculenta) is a wild berry that grows exclusively in the middle Himalayan belt - Kumaon, Garhwal, and nowhere else. It ripens in April on hillsides between 1,000 and 2,200 metres. Every Kumaoni knows the old folk song: "Kafal Pakyo Meel Nahin Chakyo." The kafal ripened but nobody was there to taste it.

The bees forage on kafal flowers in spring. The honey-to-plant connection is real, not manufactured. We named the brand for the berry because, like this honey, you can only get it if you know where to look.

How it reaches you

Four steps, and we stop there.

01 - Kumaon

One apiary

All Kaafal honey comes from a single apiary in the Kumaon region of Uttarakhand. The GPS is on our website. We will never blend across regions or claim otherwise.

02 - Harvest

Raw extraction

Honey is extracted by our named beekeeper and never heated above 40°C. Cold-extracted, minimally filtered - the pollen and natural enzymes stay intact.

03 - Lab

NMR tested

Every batch is NMR-tested at an independent lab. The full report is published at kaafal.in and linked via QR code on every jar. In 2020, 77% of major honey brands failed this test.

04 - Your table

Glass, always

Packed in glass jars with a gold metal lid. No plastic. No compromise on the container for a product that health-conscious buyers are trusting with their families.
